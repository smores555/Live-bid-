Base summary

Junior CA is the most junior pilot sitting in the seat once the bid settles — the seniority it takes to hold Captain at that base, including pilots who were already there. Junior upgrade is the most junior pilot who came from a First Officer seat and was awarded Captain in this bid. A base can post upgrades and still show a more senior junior-CA number when the junior Captains held the seat before bidding opened. Junior FO is the most junior pilot sitting in the First Officer seat once the bid settles. Open FO is the number of First Officer seats still unfilled after the bid (Open CA appears only if a Captain seat is open).
