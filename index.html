Captain upgrades by base

Junior CA is the most junior pilot sitting in the seat once the bid settles — the seniority it takes to hold Captain at that base, including pilots who were already there. Junior upgrade is the most junior pilot who came from a First Officer seat and was awarded Captain in this bid. A base can post upgrades and still show a more senior junior-CA number when the junior Captains held the seat before bidding opened.
